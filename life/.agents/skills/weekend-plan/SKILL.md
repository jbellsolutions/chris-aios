# Weekend plan

1. Work out the city: the one he named most recently in `vault/` or this chat. If unsure, use the last one and say so on the first line.
2. Read `me.md` for his favorites, budget and never-again list.
3. Search the web for what's on this Friday to Sunday in that city. Use only places and events you can see are real and open; anything you can't confirm today is marked "check before going".
4. Write three options:
   - **Big night:** dinner plus where to go after, or an event.
   - **Easy night:** low-key, good food or a bar with a vibe.
   - **Daytime:** brunch, beach, a day trip or an activity.
   Each: day, local time, price level ($ to $$$$), the reservation or ticket link, and a backup if it's full.
5. Under 120 words, the pick first. Save it to `vault/<date>.md` under "## Weekend plan".

Never book, reserve or pay. If he says "book it", give him the link and what to choose.
