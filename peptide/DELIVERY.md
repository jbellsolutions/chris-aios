# Delivering a build-out, the same way every time

When a client pays, the Peptide Operator opens `vault/clients/<client>/TRACKER.md` from the list below and works it top to bottom. Every step has an owner and a check. A person signs, pays, applies and orders; the AIOS prepares everything else. A proposed starting point, so Chris and his closer only sell; Chris and Justin adjust the steps before the first client.

| # | Step | Owner | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome message and kickoff call booked (drafts for Chris's yes) | AIOS → Chris | The kickoff is on the calendar |
| 2 | Brand questionnaire: name, look, audience, market, budget | Client, prompted by the AIOS | Answers saved to the tracker |
| 3 | Business basics checklist: entity, EIN, business bank account, insurance, their own lawyer's review of the model | Client | Each item ticked, with who confirmed it |
| 4 | Domain and site: from the brand answers, every page through `COMPLIANCE.md` | AIOS builds, client approves | Live on their domain after the client's yes |
| 5 | Supplier: picked from the approved list, certificates of analysis on file for every product listed | AIOS prepares, client orders | COAs saved; first order placed by the client |
| 6 | Payment processor: application packet prepared, client applies | AIOS prepares, client signs | Approved, and a test transaction refunded |
| 7 | Policies: terms, privacy, shipping, refunds, the product-use language the lawyer approves | AIOS drafts, client's lawyer approves | Published |
| 8 | Launch plan: channels, first 30 days, budget, the numbers to watch | AIOS writes, Chris reviews | Client has it and a launch date |
| 9 | Launch and first-30-days check-ins | AIOS reports weekly, Chris or the team calls | Day 30 review written |

The tracker's status line feeds Chris's morning pulse: who's on which step, who's stuck, and what's waiting on whom.
