# Your day with your AIOS

You talk; it works. Open the ChatGPT app → Codex → `~/chris-aios`, hold your Wispr Flow key, and say what you need.

**Every morning**
- **8:00 AM:** the COG report: yesterday's numbers, who missed EODs, what doesn't add up.
- **8:30 AM:** your brief: today's calendar, emails that need you, and what's waiting on you, decisions first. Reply "yes", "no" or "change X" to each draft.

**Every night**
- **11:30 PM and 12:45 AM:** the EOD check. In the first week it sends you the bump as a draft; reply "right" or "wrong: David submitted at 11:10". Once you approve the wording, it posts the bump itself, only for missing reps, only once.

**Any time, just say it**
- "Who's missing EODs?" · "How did we do this week?" · "Review John's call from today" · "Draft a follow-up to this lead"
- "Add this to my dream map: …" (every idea gets kept and built in order)
- "Build me a new agent for WeScale" (it walks you through it; you answer, it builds)
- Personal: start a Codex task in `~/chris-life` and ask "What should I do this weekend?" or "How much free cash do I have?"
- Peptide: start a Codex task in `~/chris-peptide` and ask "Where's the funnel stuck?" or "Start the tracker for a new client"

**Rules you can count on**
- Nothing goes out without your yes, except the EOD bump you approve yourself.
- Numbers it can't read say UNKNOWN, never zero.
- "Stop everything" stops every outward action until you say go.

**When something looks off:** say "what broke?". It checks its run log and connections and tells you the one thing it needs from you, usually a re-login in Codex's apps.
